- Headline: 5-10 words. Benefit-led, not clever.
- Description: 1 short supporting sentence under the headline.
- CTA: one of — Learn More / Book Now / Contact Us / Shop Now / Get Offer / Call Now / Send Message / Sign Up

- Visual concept: one sentence describing the ideal image or short video.

LANGUAGE: Generate all text in the language specified in the Campaign Brief. If not specified, use Greek.

- Greek audiences respond to warmth, local pride, and community trust.
